Your account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and enter the email you signed up with. We'll email you a link to set a new one. If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Profile tab and tap Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your rankings, your Want to Try list, and your follows. It can't be undone.

If you can't sign in and want your account deleted, email us from the address on the account and we'll delete it for you.

Can I change my username?

Not in the app yet. Email us with the username you'd like and we'll change it if it's available.

Safety: blocking and reporting

How do I block someone?

Open their profile, tap the ⋯ menu in the top corner, and choose Block User. Once you block someone:

  • They can no longer see your profile or your rankings, and they can't follow you.
  • Any follow between the two of you, in either direction, is removed.
  • You won't see their rankings.
  • They aren't notified.

How do I unblock someone?

Go to the Profile tab and tap Blocked accounts. You can unblock anyone from that list.

How do I report someone?

Open their profile, tap the ⋯ menu, choose Report User, and pick a reason. Reports are private: the person you report isn't told who reported them. We review every report and may remove content or accounts that break our Terms of Service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Products

A product I use isn't in Potions.

Search for it on the Search tab. If it doesn't show up, you can add it yourself from the same screen.

A product has the wrong photo or details.

Email us with the product name and what's wrong, and we'll fix it.
