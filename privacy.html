Potions Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026
Last updated: September 27, 2026

Potions ("Potions," "we," "us," or "our") operates a mobile application that lets users rank, review, and discover beauty products, and connect with other users around shared taste (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This policy also covers our website, including the waitlist sign-up form on our home page.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and the username you choose. Authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase.

Content you create. This includes the products you rank, the tiers or ratings you assign, items you add to a wishlist, and the accounts you follow or that follow you. Depending on your settings, this content may be visible to other users of the App.

Product photos you suggest. If you suggest a photo for a product, we store the image you upload and your account ID. Suggested photos are private until our team reviews them; if approved, the photo is shown to all users as that product's image (not linked to your name). Rejected photos are deleted.

Reports and blocks. If you report or block another user, we store that report or block, including the reason and any details you provide. The person you report is not told who reported them.

Waitlist. If you join our waitlist on our website, we collect your email address and, if you arrived through a tagged link, the name of that link's source (for example, "instagram"). To prevent spam we also store a one-way, salted hash of your IP address — not the address itself. We use your email only to tell you when Potions launches and about major news, and every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.

Usage data. We collect information about how you interact with the App — screens viewed, features used, and general engagement patterns — through our analytics provider, PostHog. This helps us understand what's working and what isn't.

Diagnostic and crash data. We use Sentry to automatically collect crash reports and error logs so we can identify and fix bugs. This may include device type, operating system version, and the state of the app when an error occurred.

Product catalog data. Product names, images, and brand information shown in the App are sourced from public retailer and press listings. We do not send any of your personal information to this service — it is used solely to look up product information, not to identify you.

Information we do not collect. We do not collect your precise location, and we do not access your camera, contacts, or photo library except when you explicitly choose to upload a photo (for example, a profile picture or a product photo suggestion), and only the file you select is accessed.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Operate the core features of the App — rankings, wishlists, following, and discovery
  • Diagnose and fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns so we can improve the App
  • Communicate with you about your account or significant changes to the App
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the App, and only as needed for them to provide that service:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database hosting
  • PostHog — product analytics
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting

These providers are contractually restricted from using your information for any purpose other than providing services to us.

Other users. If you use social features (following, public rankings, wishlists), that content is visible to other users according to your privacy settings within the App.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

Business transfers. If Potions is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Future Monetization

Potions may in the future include affiliate links to retailers (meaning we may earn a commission if you purchase a product through a link in the App) and may display advertising. If and when these features launch, they will not involve selling your personal information to advertisers — affiliate and ad relationships are based on aggregate or non-personal data, such as which products are popular. We will update this policy before any such feature goes live if the data practices described above change.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or security purposes.

We keep waitlist emails until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove you, or until we stop running the waitlist, whichever comes first.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Access and deletion. You can request a copy of your data, or request that we delete your account and associated data, by contacting us at ellen.waters@icloud.com.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

European users (GDPR). If you are located in the European Economic Area or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and the right to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

New York residents. We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information, consistent with New York's SHIELD Act. In the event of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected New York residents in accordance with New York law, generally within 30 days of discovering the breach.

7. Children's Privacy

Potions is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under this age, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at ellen.waters@icloud.com.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encryption in transit and access controls on our database, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process and store data in the United States or other countries. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at ellen.waters@icloud.com.
